# Task 07:

P = 8192 and the required scaled speedup is at least 7500.

\[8192-\alpha(8191)\geq7500\]
\[\alpha\leq\frac{8192-7500}{8191}=\frac{692}{8191}\]

\[\alpha_{max}\approx0.084483\]
The largest allowed serial fraction is about 8.45%. A larger fraction would make the predicted speedup fall below 7500.
